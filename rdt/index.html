🐥🫧🛁 Rubber Duck Talks

A weekly accountability pairing system.


How it works:

  1. Submit your name and email below to be included
  2. Every Sunday morning, you'll get an RDT reminder email which includes a link to opt out
  3. Monday noon: you'll get your RDT pairing for the week
  4. You and your RDT partner can schedule your own check-in for the week...
  5. ...and repeat! The system is set up to allow repeat pairs only if they haven't occurred in two consecutive weeks (e.g., John and Jane get paired up this week; they can't be paired up again until the week after next).
