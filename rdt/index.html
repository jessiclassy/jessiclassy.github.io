🐥🫧🛁 Rubber Duck Talks

A weekly accountability pairing system.


How it works:

  1. Submit your name and email below to be included
  2. Every Sunday morning, you'll get an RDT reminder email which includes a link to opt out
  3. Monday noon: you'll get your RDT pairing for the week
  4. You and your RDT partner can schedule your own check-in for the week...
  5. ...and repeat! The system is set up to randomly generate pairs, only regenerating pairs if there is a repeated pair from the previous week (e.g., John and Jane get paired up this week; they can't be paired up again until the week after next).
  6. If there are an odd number of people opted in, there will be one group of three.
